# Privacy Policy

Tone Desk (formerly Color Studio) is a free, open source app by Bernardo Ferrari. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and it doesn't collect personal information.

## What stays on your device

- **Your theme.** The color, style, knobs and mixer settings are saved on your device so the desk is the way you left it. Clearing the app's data (or the site's data in your browser) removes them.
- **Photos.** When you pick a color from a photo, the photo is read on your device to find its colors. It is never uploaded or kept.
- **Exports.** Copying code puts it on your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## What reaches the internet

- **Fonts.** Tone Desk downloads its typefaces from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com). Like any download, that request shows Google your IP address. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **The web version** is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com), which may log visits as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Links** in the About dialog open GitHub in your browser.

## Children

Tone Desk is suitable for everyone and knowingly collects nothing from anyone, children included.
